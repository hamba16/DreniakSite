## PART B — Conceptual imagery: where it's needed, and what it should show

### Why this matters
Several sections on both divisions are currently icon-and-text only with no supporting imagery at all (most visibly the Sectors pages on both divisions, and the Insights/Projects hero areas). The brand's own guideline establishes a clear photography treatment — duotone, grayscale base tinted in the division's brand color — demonstrated on the approved pull-up banner (construction-site photography, red-tinted for one variant, indigo for the other). Nothing generated so far has extended that treatment beyond the two conceptual images used at launch. This pass commissions a proper set.

**Non-negotiable honesty rule, carried over from the original build:** every generated image is illustrative/conceptual, evoking the *kind* of work Dreniak does, never presented as a photograph of a real, named, completed Dreniak project. Caption or alt-text framing should stay generic ("infrastructure inspection," "urban transport corridor") rather than implying a specific real project, client, or location is depicted. This matters doubly here because the concepts below are informed by real, current Uganda infrastructure activity — the images should feel unmistakably Ugandan in character without claiming to depict any specific real scheme.

### Grounding research: what "modern Ugandan infrastructure" actually looks like right now
Pulled from current reporting so the imagery reads as informed rather than generic "Africa" stock photography:

- **Roads & urban transport:** Kampala is mid-way through a citywide road, junction, and drainage upgrade programme, alongside flyover/interchange construction easing congestion at major junctions. The Kampala–Jinja Expressway is under active construction as a high-speed toll corridor. Tarmac highway expansion continues nationwide under UNRA.
- **Rail:** Construction began in 2026 on the Kampala–Malaba Standard Gauge Railway — notably electric-traction from day one, connecting toward Kenya's rail network. This is a genuinely modern (electrified, not diesel-legacy) rail image opportunity.
- **Energy:** Hydropower (Karuma, Isimba) underpins the grid; transmission-line and substation expansion is active (including cross-border lines toward South Sudan); solar and rural electrification continue to expand access.
- **Ports & logistics:** Bukasa Port on Lake Victoria is under development as a modern inland-water logistics hub.
- **Buildings:** Kampala's commercial high-rise boom is trending toward more technically sophisticated buildings — proper HVAC, lifts, energy efficiency, logistics docks — i.e. buildings that need genuine asset/lifecycle management, not just a facade.
- **Engineering practice itself:** A government minister publicly urged engineers in 2026 to move past outdated manual practices ("stop chipping concrete to taste its strength") toward modern methods and technology — a strong, real signal that modern instrumentation, testing, and digital methods are an active national conversation, not an imported aesthetic.

Use this as the mood board, not a shot list of real projects to reproduce. Every image below is a *generic scene inspired by these themes*, not a depiction of any specific named project.

### Style specification (apply to every image above)
- Duotone treatment: grayscale base, tinted in Business Red `#991923` for Engineering surfaces or Indigo `#0d3251` for Asset Management surfaces — matching the approved pull-up banner treatment exactly, not a generic sepia or arbitrary color grade.
- "Modern but reasonable technology" — ground images in plausible, current construction/engineering practice (tablets, drones for site inspection, standard modern machinery, structural sensors, digital plan review) rather than science-fiction or overtly futuristic visual tropes (no holographic UI, no implausible robotics). The goal is credible near-term modernity, not speculative fiction.
- People, where shown, should read as professional and engaged in real work (site engineers, supervisors, technicians) rather than posed stock-photo "handshake" imagery — consistent with the tone guidance already established in the original build brief (avoid engineering-firm clichés).
- Consistent light quality/mood across the set so it reads as one coherent photographic library rather than mismatched individual generations — establish one lighting/time-of-day approach (the existing pull-up banner reference leans toward clear, slightly overcast daylight) and apply it across the set.
- Export as optimized WebP, matching the format already used for the two existing conceptual images, sized appropriately for both the desktop and mobile layouts already established.

### What NOT to include
- No text, logos, or watermarks baked into the generated images themselves (the motif/logo overlay is applied separately in the UI, per the existing pattern).
- No identifiable real people, no real company signage or logos, no depiction implying a specific real, named project or client.
- No sector image that contradicts the confirmed service/sector scope in `ENGINEERING_UPDATE_BRIEF.md` — e.g. nothing depicting mining or agricultural machinery as an active Dreniak service line, since those appeared only in the SEO keyword list, not the confirmed services (see that brief's Section 9 note).
